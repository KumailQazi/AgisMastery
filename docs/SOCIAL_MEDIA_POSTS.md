> 🌟 **94.2% of employees complete compliance training. Only 58.4% make the right decision during an actual crisis.** That 35.8% "Mastery Gap" is where costly security breaches and system outages happen.
> 
> Today, our team is proud to introduce **AgisMastery**, built for the **#AllThingsAgentic Hackathon** by Google & Devpost! 🚀
> 
> Instead of passive slide decks and predictable quizzes, **AgisMastery** immerses learners in high-pressure workplace crisis simulations:
> 
> 🔹 **Google ADK Socratic Orchestrator:** Powered by **Gemini 3.5**, the agent never gives away the answer. It engages in a multi-turn Socratic dialogue to challenge assumptions and build resilient decision frameworks.
> 🔹 **Silent Cognitive Telemetry:** Our background engine passively logs millisecond-level hesitation, hint consumption, and cognitive friction to generate actionable **Struggle Heatmaps** for instructional designers.
> 🔹 **Enterprise Zero-Trust (Gemma + Model Armor):** Sensitive corporate reflections are processed on-prem via local Gemma with real-time Google Model Armor PII redaction.
> 🔹 **Multimodal Accessibility:** Google Veo 8-second visual crisis sequences + Google Lyria cognitive load-adaptive voice coaching.
> 
> Built with **Google ADK**, **FastAPI**, **React**, **Google Cloud Run**, and **Firestore**.
